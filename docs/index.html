Why it slaps

BUILT FOR
REAL GAMERS

🎮

2,500+ Games

A massive pre-loaded database spanning every genre — from AAA blockbusters to hidden indie gems. Find any game instantly.

🔍

Instant Search

Animated search box with live filtering. Full keyboard support including Ctrl shortcuts, word-jump, and text selection.

📑

8 Smart Tabs

All, Wishlist, Played, Playing, Finished, Dropped, Favourites, Rotation. Your library, always perfectly organised.

⚡

Lightning Fast

SDL2-powered native desktop app. No Electron, no browser, no bloat. Smooth 60fps rendering even with thousands of entries.

🖼️

List & Grid View

Switch between a dense list layout and a visual grid. Sort by name or year, ascending or descending.

💾

Auto-Save

Your progress is saved automatically to a local binary file. No cloud, no account — just your data on your machine.

🎨

Multiple Themes

Cycle through built-in colour themes on the fly with [ ] keys. Find a look that fits your setup.

🖥️

Custom Window

Frameless borderless design with custom title bar, drag-to-resize from any edge, and smooth maximise animation.

Track Your Progress

IN · Playing

Currently in progress. Your active session tracker.

FN · Finished

Credits rolled. Main story done. Well earned.

DR · Dropped

FV · Favourite

The all-timers. Games that left a mark.

RT · Rotation

Games you return to repeatedly. Your comfort picks.
